    How ranking and contribution work

    Improve your best. Don’t grind the total.

    Your balanced score is (best score against rival A + best score against rival B) ÷ 2. An unplayed rival counts as zero. Only completed, account-owned, server-verified standard operations qualify. Verified defeats and timeouts score zero.

    Repeating a matchup replaces your best only when it improves. Practice, tutorials, abandoned, pending, rejected and old anonymous runs never add points.

    A faction is twenty places.

    We add the top 20 balanced player scores in each faction and divide by 20. Empty places score zero. If you’re in the top 20, your contribution is your balanced score ÷ 20. Below the cut, your contribution is zero until you move up.

    The race stays provisional until every faction has five ranked players. This is a local test season with no scheduled reset. Different gameplay-rule versions are kept separate.

    Ties are predictable.

    For each rival: higher score, then more home integrity, fewer simulation ticks, earlier ticket, then stable match ID. For player ties: more combined home integrity, fewer combined ticks (an unplayed rival comes last), earlier start of the last counted run, then stable account ID.

    Equal faction totals share a rank. These are asynchronous operations against AI. This score formula is transparent; it does not establish equal competitive difficulty or prevent multiple accounts.
