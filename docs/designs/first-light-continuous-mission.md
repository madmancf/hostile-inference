# First Light: a continuous mission in the existing game

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-11-06. Repo: madmancf/hostile-inference. Branch: publish-runtime-assets. Status: approved for local implementation on 2026-11-06. Pushing and deployment remain excluded. Mode: Builder.

## Purpose

Make the existing game immediately understandable and worth another match for people following the AI race, especially classic RTS fans. The desired reaction is that it looks amazing, plays well and makes the player want to compete for a faction. Developers can build on that player experience.

The working game is the design source of truth. Reuse its interface, art, maps, animations and controls. The selected Field orders concept expresses a preference for battlefield visibility and concise instructions; it does not mandate replacing the HUD or copying generated imagery. The owner's subsequent request to use the working design controls this brief. Extra design opinions were declined.

## Premises and boundaries

- Prove the existing three-faction experience from first order through a complete operation before adding systems.
- Teach the AI-lab theme through tactical consequences while preserving every building, technology, unit and counter learning objective.
- Explain outcomes, personal improvement and actual faction contribution without changing the approved scoring rule.
- Keep full graphics and the 60 FPS target. Certification, hosting, email/domain setup and deployment remain deferred; this brief does not waive them.
- Retain guest training/practice, existing account flows, faction allegiance, verified-only standings and the 15-minute operation cap. Training remains untimed. This is solo play against AI with asynchronous competition.

## Chosen experience

One First Light mission connects **defend, sustain and breach** on the existing faction campuses. Units, damage, resource consequences and paid choices persist between chapters. There is no reset into a separate tutorial after the opening fight.

| Chapter | Player experience | Existing objectives retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defend | Inspect home, select a small field team and use the faction's defining tactic against a controlled threat. | campus, formation |
| Sustain | See why the team needs staff, energy, cooling and compute; build and research a chosen response. | staff, battery, cooling, cloud, build, research, expand, specialize, models, malware |
| Breach | Read the selected rival's counter, transfer the team, disable supporting technology and finish the endpoint. | counter, transfer, recon, sabotage, endpoint |

The opening teaches actual faction differences using existing mechanics:

- **Misanthropic:** hold a Constitution still to establish its ward, keep an Oracle inside, and use Quarantine. Show that moving breaks the ward.
- **Closed AI:** keep a Black Box linked to a Gatekeeper, concentrate on one target and use the linked model ability. Explain a broken link before asking the player to recover it; teach Broker marks later with the agent.
- **Broke Labs:** use No Filter's burst, observe its recovery and rising heat, then connect that consequence to anchoring the cooling vessel and using Cold Take. Do not silently cool the campus for the player.

Use tutorial-authored starting positions, supplies and encounter timing, with ordinary unit mechanics. Keep the initial team small and separated; stage later roles out of the encounter. Instructions and threat release wait for deliberate player input. Aim for a meaningful faction action within 90 seconds of active play; this is a playtest hypothesis, not a measured result. Preserve the complete formation curriculum even where it spans chapters, including Broke's burst, recovery and cooling sequence.

For a lost teaching unit or failed encounter, offer an explicit retry from a training checkpoint instead of leaving an impossible objective. Save the checkpoint before releasing the threat; a retry clearly restores that encounter's units, wallet and progress. Normal chapter transitions do not restore spent resources or lost units. Do not grant ranked rewards for training or silently auto-complete a teaching action.

## Use the working interface

Reuse the mission panel, selection dock, existing tabs, map focus, compact health indicators, optional explanations and debrief. Show one current objective, one next order and a visible completion condition. Keep the selected unit and its valid commands readable at 100% zoom. Describe a blocked order and its remedy, such as selecting a model that lacks a Gatekeeper link.

Start with copy, ordering, camera framing and existing disclosure controls. Reflow an existing component only when a concrete overlap or readability failure demonstrates the need. A new overlay, global palette/font change, replacement HUD, new faction art and a renderer rewrite are outside this work. Identify the focal friendly and hostile units using current assets and restrained markers; avoid repeated floating names over crowds. Keep combat visible while the player reads.

## Outcome and next match

Improve the existing debrief: state victory/defeat, name the relevant tactical outcome supported by recorded events, and suggest one actionable next attempt. Never invent a causal explanation unsupported by the run. Training explicitly earns no faction points. Practice retains the trained faction; guest sign-up carries that faction as a preference, without overriding an existing account after login.

For a signed-in player whose allegiance differs from the trained faction, visibly name both destinations before starting: for example, “Practice Misanthropic” and “Play for Closed AI,” with an explanation that verified play uses the account's allegiance. Never silently change membership or launch verified play as the trained faction. If allegiance is still changeable, link to the existing account faction-choice flow and require the player's explicit selection there; otherwise explain that it is locked. Re-read the account after returning and label the verified action with its actual faction. Matching-faction accounts use the ordinary handoff.

For verified operations, wait for the existing authoritative result and read the current standings. Show personal bests against both rivals, current rank and counted faction contribution, including zero below the top 20. Suggest an unplayed rival or an existing best to improve; do not promise a future rank. Pending, rejected, disconnected or unavailable verification must not display provisional faction points. Preserve retries and access to prior results.

The rule stays unchanged: average each player's best verified score against the two rivals, treating an unplayed rival as zero; count the faction's top 20 players and divide by 20, with empty slots zero. Repeated runs cannot accumulate extra points by grinding.

## Implementation and compatibility

Work primarily in tutorial.js, game.js, the existing demo markup/styles and their tests. Use the existing faction rules, facilities, tech tree, authored territories and result APIs. Carry stable objective identifiers into a versioned tutorial-state migration. Map every currently supported legacy save and version-2 lesson to equivalent progress; preserve units, HP, wallet, paid work, completed research, final specialization and an active raid. Later saves do not replay the new opening. Migration must be idempotent and must not overwrite an unreadable original save.

The tutorial and UI are outside the ranked simulation's frozen import closure, but the frozen engine applies an energy floor and heat cap whenever tutorial.step is below 3. Avoid that legacy behavior explicitly: version-3 tutorial state keeps the engine-compatibility step at the named constant 3 and tracks actual progress separately through stable objectiveId values and completed objectives. Update tutorial/UI/save-validation consumers to use those identifiers instead of treating step as a sequence index. Chapter ordering must never reactivate the old clamp. This is a documented compatibility adapter, not a change to engine.js.

Before the first simulation tick, migrate every supported legacy or version-2 save by mapping its old lesson to objectiveId, preserving its completed learning and paid state, then assigning the compatibility step. Fresh tutorials use the same representation from creation. Keep the frozen import closure and leaderboard policy byte-identical. If another shared-simulation dependency prevents this adapter, stop for a separate compatibility decision; never change the rules hash merely to pass tests. No database migration or new service is planned.

## Evidence and success criteria

Current source has 17 learning objectives and an existing debrief. Three independent review calls (Claude Code, Argon 4 High and Opus 5.5 High through agy) supported testing earlier faction combat and reuse. They represent two model families, not three. The owner chose a continuous mission over their proposed separate introduction to avoid repetition. None proves enjoyment or an ideal tutorial duration. Public OpenRA, Chrono Divide and Beyond All Reason materials informed familiar controls and staged instruction; they are reference patterns, not retention evidence.

Acceptance for the eventual implementation:

1. Every retained objective completes across all six faction/rival pairings; valid opening, support and final technology choices remain reachable. Tests reject wrong-unit, wrong-continent and unrelated-ability completion, cover failed-encounter recovery, and round-trip saves during construction, research, transit and later chapters. Fresh and migrated state must avoid the legacy resource guard in every objective: a tick at heat 60 and energy 70 follows ordinary mechanics, never a forced reset to heat 45 or energy 80. Test before/after save loading and chapter transitions, including the Broke burst-to-cooling sequence.
2. At 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1920×1080, 100% zoom, no required command is clipped or hidden behind the battlefield. Existing keyboard selection, movement, ability, pause and focus controls work. Browser checks stay in background Chromium or Chrome for Testing with isolated data.
3. Training/practice cannot affect standings. Verify guest, matching-account and mismatched-account handoffs, with both locked and changeable allegiance; labels, practice faction, explicit account choice and the verified ticket must agree. Verified results, retries, first rival, both rivals, personal-best improvement, unchanged best and zero top-20 contribution remain truthful. Ranked rule fingerprints remain identical.
4. Observe six first-time players, two per faction. Proposed bar: at least five can execute the opening tactic within 90 seconds of active play and explain their next order without coaching. Observe at least one full tutorial and subsequent operation per faction; record confusion, return intent and visual feedback separately from automated success. These results are not yet available.
5. Retain full-quality rendering. Record regressions and hitches; certify 60 FPS later in the agreed quiet-window test. Passing functional tests does not certify performance or creative quality.
