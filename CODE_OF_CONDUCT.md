# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Critique code and game design, not people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing and spam are not welcome. Keep discussion relevant
and do not post private information or flood issues with unreviewed model output.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads,
or restrict participation. Use GitHub's private **Report content** control to
report abuse; use `SECURITY.md` for vulnerabilities. Do not repost harmful material
or private details to make a public report.
