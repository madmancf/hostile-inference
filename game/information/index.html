Competition rules

Season Zero is ongoing, with no scheduled end. Each verified operation lasts at most 15 minutes. Training and practice never award public points. Starting your first verified operation commits your account to its faction for this season.

Your balanced score is your best verified score against each rival, added together and divided by two. An unplayed rival counts as zero. Your faction’s score is its top 20 balanced scores divided by 20; empty places count as zero. Repeating a run can improve a best score but cannot accumulate extra points.

Player ties use remaining headquarters health, completion ticks, time the run was issued, then a stable account order. Factions with equal points share a rank. The server reproduces accepted commands under frozen rules. Only committed, verified results from eligible accounts count. A service fault leaves a result pending; it does not label you a cheater.

No account farming, impersonation, offensive callsigns, score tampering or attempts to disrupt the service. Report concerns from a player’s standings row. Reports go to a private review queue and do not automatically punish a player. An operator can rename or suspend an account, excluding its points while suspended. Restoring the account restores eligible points. Verification proves legal recorded commands, not a human behind the keyboard or one person per account.

This local build admits eight unfinished operations at a time, with up to 60 starts per hour. When it is full, retry later or play practice. Public capacity has not been certified.

Your profile & data

Your callsign, faction, verified scores, rank and contribution are public on standings. Your username, recovery address, password, recovery code, sessions and detailed command history are private. Reports use an opaque public reference, not your private account ID.

Passwords and recovery codes are hashed. Email links expire after 30 minutes and work once. Setting a recovery address requires your password; it cannot recover an account until verified. A successful reset signs out previous sessions and replaces the recovery code. When configured, Resend processes the recipient address and recovery email content for delivery. Unconfigured local simulation keeps links in the private operator outbox and sends no email. Keep your recovery code: losing it and your password without a verified email leaves no self-service recovery.

Account access uses a strictly necessary session cookie. Sessions expire after seven idle days or 30 days from sign-in. Game assets and practice progress are cached on your device. There are no advertising or analytics trackers.

Abuse counters use keyed source identifiers, retained for up to 24 hours; raw client addresses are not saved by these controls. Private reports and moderation reasons expire after 90 days. Recovery links and their local outbox records expire after 30 minutes. Queued delivery content is cleared after provider acceptance, cancellation or final failure; minimal delivery status records expire after seven days. Provider-side retention is separate. Operator access to this build is local to the machine.

You can delete your account from Account access after accepted operations settle. Deletion immediately revokes sessions, removes your public rank and contribution, and removes your username, callsign, email and credentials. Unidentified match records remain for competition integrity. The proposed launch backup policy retains private backups for at most 30 days; automated rotation and off-host storage must be configured and verified before publication. Existing local safety backups have not been deleted.

Support & review

Signed-in players can use “Report a player or ask for help” in their account. It creates a private local ticket with a reference number. Operators review it using the private administration tool. No message is sent to an external service.

If you cannot sign in, use your saved recovery code or a previously verified recovery address. This local candidate has no public support email or recovery delivery provider yet. Those contact and delivery details must be connected and tested before opening public sign-ups. No recovery is granted merely from a username or callsign.
