     FIELD MANUAL

    A few good orders.

    Click a friendly unit to select. Shift-click adds units. Drag a box to select a group. Right-click a site to move, harvest or dock; an enemy to attack. With a selection, click empty ground to move.

    The selection dock shows orders for the current unit. The Units tab selects your forces; Intel explains the selection; Systems contains your faction buildings, research tree and doctrine. Each facility unlocks a research branch; choose one final specialization. Destroy an enemy facility to disable its bonuses. Rebuilding restores completed research. “Attack server” transfers your models, agents and firewalls through the home uplink. In operations, select the units you want to transfer; Shift-click a selected unit to leave it defending home. Research and supply stay at home. New units deploy at campus or dock. Secure the rival data relay for 30 seconds to weaken endpoint shielding. Right-click a rival facility to sabotage it. Construction and research share a campus queue; research needs staff at the lab. Cancelling refunds unused work. Your faction doctrine grants a 20-second tactical window.

    1–8 deploy units · Tab cycle selection · Space pause · H campus · F whole map · C clean camera · E selected ability · S hold position. Wheel to zoom. Middle-drag to pan.

    Firewalls contain malware and block researcher lockouts. Researchers validate and repair. Models project inference attacks. Cooling ships and floating clouds use water routes.
